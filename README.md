# ShippersLab Landing

Landing page for ShippersLab. Built to show who we are, what we build and where we're heading. 🦐

Everything here is the public face of ShippersLab: the site that introduces our services, showcases our projects, highlights upcoming events and gives a spotlight to the sponsors that make it all possible.

### What's inside

- `services/` —> what we offer and how we work
- `projects/` —> the things we're building and shipping
- `events/` —> meetups, workshops and community gatherings we're organizing
- `sponsors/` —> the people and companies backing ShippersLab

Studio, lab and community. 💫
